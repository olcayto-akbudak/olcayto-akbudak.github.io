About

An analyst who connects the layers.

For more than four years, I have worked in ERP and Turkey’s mandatory e-document ecosystem: electronic invoicing, archiving and waybills governed by the Turkish Revenue Administration (GİB). I weigh stakeholder needs against regulatory and system constraints, then translate them into implementable requirements and acceptance scenarios.

I trace problems beyond the screen. When needed, I query databases with SQL, inspect REST/SOAP responses and UBL/XML documents, and document findings that business units and development teams can act on.

Code reading, prototypes and automation support my analysis. My professional focus is business and systems analysis: understanding the workflow, clarifying requirements and validating the proposed solution.

Method

How I work

  1. Understand the need.Listen to stakeholders and separate the problem from the requested solution.
  2. Map the process.Model the current flow with its business rules, regulatory requirements and system constraints.
  3. Validate with data.Test assumptions through SQL queries, document samples and API responses.
  4. Close the loop.Refine the requirement, verify acceptance scenarios and track findings through resolution.

Featured work

Public event-log dataset / Process analysis

Purchase-to-pay process mining

Analysis of waiting time, payment blocks and goods-receipt exceptions across 1.6 million SAP event-log entries from the public BPI Challenge 2019 dataset. Within-vendor comparison helps distinguish apparent delay from controllable delay.

Key finding: Of the 655 items flagged by the “payment without goods receipt” rule, 585 were correction flows. In all 70 remaining exceptions, the payment block had been lifted manually.
